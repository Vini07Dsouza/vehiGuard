## 1. What is this project?

VehiLence is a final-year college project. It reads a vehicle's number plate with a camera, checks it against a **dummy database**, and checks whether the driver's face matches the registered owner. It is a classroom prototype. It is **not** connected to the police, to any government system, or to any real database.

## 2. Why am I being asked?

I need a small number of volunteer faces to build and test the face-matching part of the project.

## 3. What will be collected?

- **Face photos** of you: about 3 to 5 photos, taken in different lighting and angles.
- A **code** (for example V01) instead of your name in project files.
- Your **role** in the test data (see section 4).

I will not collect your address, ID numbers, phone number or any other personal details for the project.

## 4. How will my photos be used?

Your photos will be turned into a numerical "face signature" (an embedding) and used only to test the system. You may be assigned one of these **make-believe roles** in the test database:

- Vehicle owner
- Family member or other driver
- A person on a **fictional** "wanted list"
- A person on a **fictional** "missing persons" list

**These roles are pretend.** They do not mean you are wanted, missing or suspected of anything. No real police record is created.

## 5. Where will my data be stored, and who can see it?

- Photos and face signatures are stored **only on my project laptop** (and a private backup, if I make one).
- They will **not** be uploaded to GitHub, shared on social media or sent to anyone else.
- Only I, and my project guide if needed for evaluation, can see them.
- Photos may appear in the report, slides or demo **only if you tick "Yes" in section 7**. Otherwise they will not.

## 6. How long will my data be kept?

Your photos and face signatures will be **deleted within 30 days after the final project submission and evaluation**, or earlier if you ask.

## 8. Your rights

- Taking part is **voluntary**. You can say no without giving a reason.
- You can **withdraw at any time** and ask me to delete your photos and face signature. Contact me using the details at the top, and I will delete them promptly.
- Withdrawing will not affect you in any way.

## 9. Things you should know

- Face-recognition technology is not perfect. The system may match the wrong person or fail to recognise you.
- You must be **18 years or older** to take part.
