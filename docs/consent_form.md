## 1. What is this project?

VehiGuard is a final-year college project. It uses phone cameras to read vehicle number plates, checks them against a **dummy database that I created myself**, and looks for suspicious patterns, such as the same plate appearing at two places too far apart for one vehicle to have travelled between them. It is a classroom prototype. It is **not** connected to the police, to any government system, or to any real database. It does **not** recognise or identify people.

## 2. Why am I being asked?

I need a small number of vehicles (cars, motorcycles and similar) and their number plates to build and test the plate-reading and vehicle-appearance parts of the project.

## 3. What will be collected?

- **Photos or short videos** of your vehicle, including its number plate, taken in different lighting, angles and distances.
- Basic **vehicle details**: type, make, model and colour.
- Your **plate number**, only if you agree in section 7.
- A **code** (for example V01) instead of your name in project files.
- Your vehicle's **role** in the test data (see section 4).

I will not collect your address, ID numbers, phone number, driving licence or any other personal details for the project. I will not detect, store or use faces. If a person happens to be visible in a photo or video, that person is not identified, and the face will be cropped or blurred in anything shown in the report, slides or demo.

## 4. How will my vehicle data be used?

Your photos and vehicle details will be used only to test the system. Your vehicle may be given one of these **make-believe roles** in the test database:

- A normal, genuine vehicle
- A vehicle in a **fictional** "stolen" report
- A vehicle in a **fictional** "missing" report
- The **genuine owner** of a plate in a **pretend clone** test (see below)

**These roles are pretend.** They do not mean your vehicle is stolen, missing or suspected of anything. No real police record is created.

**Pretend clone test.** To test cloned-plate detection, I may show a **printed or on-screen image** of a plate to the camera in a controlled classroom test. A copy of your plate will **never** be attached to any vehicle, shown in public or used outside the test.

## 5. Where will my data be stored, and who can see it?

- Photos, videos and the test database are stored **only on my project laptop** (and a private backup, if I make one).
- They will **not** be uploaded to GitHub, shared on social media or sent to anyone else.
- Only I, and my project guide if needed for evaluation, can see them.
- Photos of your vehicle may appear in the report, slides or demo **only if you tick "Yes" in section 7**. Otherwise they will not.

## 6. How long will my data be kept?

Your photos, videos and vehicle records will be **deleted within 30 days after the final project submission and evaluation**, or earlier if you ask.
